Penglide privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

This policy covers the Penglide app, published by Sorumatik. Questions go to info@sorumatik.co.

Short version: the app works offline and we have no servers. Your notebooks stay on your device, in the library folder you choose, in your own Android device backup if you have that turned on, and in your own Google Drive if you sign in to that backup. We never receive any of it, and we do not sell or share it. Google Play's Data safety section may still list files as collected, because the app can send them to your own Drive at your request. That does not mean they reach us. Version 1.0 on Google Play does not offer the Drive backup yet.

What the app stores, and where

  • Notebooks (handwriting, text, images, voice notes, imported PDFs), settings and a search index are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • If you choose a library folder in Settings, a copy of each notebook is written there as a single file. That folder is under your control (for example on your device storage or a folder synced by another app you chose).
  • Next to those copies the app writes a small file, Penglide library.json, with your folder and tag names, their colours and which notebook sits where, so that they come back after a restore. The names are plain text. Locked and encrypted notebooks are not listed in it.
  • An error log is kept on your device. It is meant for technical errors and does not collect notebook content, but an error message can include a file path or a notebook's title. It leaves the device only if you share it yourself from Settings. It is cut down when it grows, and deleted with the app.

Android device backup

If device backup is on, Android may copy the app's private storage to your Google account and restore it on a new device. This happens between Android and your Google account. We never receive that copy, and we cannot read it.

  • It can include notebooks, settings, the search index and the list of notebooks.
  • An encrypted notebook goes only as its encrypted file. While it is unlocked, its open copy is left out. Its title, tags and cover stay readable, as they do in the library list.
  • Android limits the size of this backup, so large libraries may not be copied at all. Do not rely on it as your only copy: use the library folder for that.
  • You can turn it off in your device's backup settings, and remove the copy from your Google account there.

Google Drive backup (optional)

This is off until you sign in with Google in Settings. Until then the app makes no network connections of its own. If you sign in:

  • The app talks only to Google: to sign you in, and to copy notebooks to a Penglide folder in your own Drive. We have no server in between, and we never receive your notebooks, your address or your access token. Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • It asks for the drive.file permission, so it can see only the files it made. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.
  • Your Google address is kept on your device, to show which account is in use.
  • Locked notebooks are not copied. A copy made before a notebook was locked stays in your Drive until you delete it there. Encrypted notebooks go only as their encrypted file, with no title in the file name. When you encrypt a notebook its file in Drive is replaced, but Drive may keep the older version for a while. The names of other notebooks appear as file names in your Drive.
  • The file Penglide library.json, described above, goes to the same folder.
  • Google may store this data outside your country. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to what is stored in your Drive.
  • To stop, turn off automatic backup or sign out in Settings. Backups stay in your Drive until you delete them there. You can remove the app's access in your Google Account.

Penglide's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the app does not do

  • It makes no network connections of its own, apart from the Drive backup above.
  • No account with us, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking. We have added no advertising or analytics SDK. Google's sign-in and Play services libraries run only if you choose to sign in, under Google's terms.
  • No access to your contacts or location.

What Google Play tells us

If you allow usage and diagnostics on your device, Google may show us crash and performance statistics for the app in the Play Console, such as device model and Android version. This comes from Google Play and not from the app, and it holds nothing from your notebooks.

Microphone and camera

  • The microphone is on only while you record a voice note, and the app shows that it is. It asks for this permission the first time you record. The recording is kept in the notebook on your device and leaves it only with that notebook, through the copies and backups described here or when you share or export it.
  • The camera is used only when you scan a document into a note.

Files you open or export

When you import a PDF or an image, or export / share a notebook, the app reads or writes only the file you pick in the system file picker or share sheet.

Links, looking things up, printing and note lock

  • A web link in a notebook opens in the browser app you use, only when you tap it. From then on the browser's own privacy terms apply.
  • When you choose Look up or translate on selected text, up to 4,000 characters of it go to the app you pick, such as a dictionary or a translator. That app's own privacy terms apply.
  • Printing hands the page to the print service you pick in the system print dialog.
  • Locked notes use the system screen-lock or biometric prompt. The app only learns whether the check passed; it never receives your fingerprint, face or PIN.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your data stays until you delete it. Delete notebooks from the library (Trash → Delete permanently), or uninstall the app to remove everything in its private storage. Copies in your library folder stay until you delete them, a device backup stays in your Google account until you remove it there, and Drive backups stay in your Drive until you delete them there. We hold no copy, so we cannot delete, restore or read any of it for you.

Your rights

If the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, No. 6698) or the EU or UK GDPR applies to you, you have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, objection, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey, the KVKK Board). Since your notebooks are only in places you control, you can use most of these yourself with the controls above. Google handles what is in your Google account as its own controller. For anything else, write to the address below.

When you write to us, we use your address and message only to answer you.

Children

The app has no accounts and we receive nothing from anyone, children included. If a child uses the Drive backup, the Google account's own rules apply.
